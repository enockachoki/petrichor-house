The scent of first rain

Petrichor is the earthy smell of rain on dry ground. Ours is a quiet white house set on green lawns, three kilometres out of Kakamega town on the Mumias road, with garden-view studios, a rooftop bar, a grill and pizzeria, and breakfast cooked the moment you want it.

Our house

A peaceful haven for rest, or for quiet business.

The Petrichor sits behind its own gates on the Kakamega–Mumias road in Shiyunzu, a short walk from Muliro Gardens and five minutes from town. Crisp white walls, black steel windows and stone pillars look out over a wide lawn, with bamboo and bougainvillea around the courtyard.

Every stay is bed & breakfast. Day guests are welcome too: meet or work in the garden, lunch on the terrace under the maroon umbrellas, then end the day with kebabs off the grill at the glass-roofed rooftop bar.
