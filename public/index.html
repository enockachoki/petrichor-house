A rainforest retreat in Shiyunzu, Kakamega

The scent of first rain

Petrichor is the earthy perfume of rain on dry ground. In Kakamega, home of Kenya's last tropical rainforest, it arrives most afternoons. We built a house around it.

Our house

Individually furnished rooms, a garden that drinks the rain, and breakfast cooked the moment you want it.

Set on quiet lawns in Shiyunzu, five minutes from Muliro Gardens and twenty from Kakamega Forest, The Petrichor pairs the warmth of a Luhya homestead with the polish of a grand hotel. Every stay includes a cooked-to-order breakfast, butler-led arrivals and fibre Wi-Fi throughout.

Mvua · Restaurant & Fireside Bar

Western Kenya, plated with ceremony

Mvua means rain in Swahili. Our kitchen cooks with what grows within a day's drive: kienyeji chicken from Isukha farms, tilapia and omena from Lake Victoria, forest honey, and the traditional greens our grandmothers called tsisaka and managu.
