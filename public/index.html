Boutique guest house · Shiyunzu, Kakamega

The scent of first rain

Petrichor is the earthy smell of rain on dry ground. Ours is a quiet house of concrete, glass and greenery, three kilometres out of Kakamega town on the Mumias road, with garden-view studios, a restaurant and pizzeria, and breakfast cooked the moment you want it.

Our house

A peaceful haven for rest, or for quiet business.

Petrichor House sits on the Kakamega–Mumias road in Shiyunzu, a short walk from Muliro Gardens and five minutes from town. High ceilings, big windows and hanging plants fill the house with light; outside, ferns spill from concrete planters around a garden terrace.

Every stay is bed & breakfast. Day guests are welcome too: use the house as a calm place to meet, work or eat, then end the afternoon with a pizza at the outdoor bar.

Restaurant · Pizzeria · Outdoor Bar

Pizza from the oven, chicken from the farm

Eat in the high-ceilinged dining hall under hanging plants, out on the garden terrace, or at the outdoor bar with the match on the screen. Our pizzeria turns out everything from a simple Margherita to the Petrichor Special, and the kitchen cooks Kenyan favourites: kienyeji chicken, Lake Victoria tilapia and nyama choma.
